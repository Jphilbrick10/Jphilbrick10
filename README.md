## I build what begins as an impossible question.

I am a creator, systems thinker, and founder of **[Coherence Energy Labs](https://coherenceenergylabs.com)**.

My work begins with questions that usually live in separate worlds:

- Can physics, computation, biology, intelligence, and meaning share a deeper organizing structure?
- Can machines produce results that are independently re-runnable, falsifiable, and provable instead of merely persuasive?
- Can software search spaces too large for ordinary human intuition and return discoveries that survive exact verification?
- Can coherence become an engineering quantity that helps us understand why systems hold together, fail, adapt, and evolve?

I am not interested in making extraordinary ideas sound ordinary. I am interested in making them **testable**.

> **If you can hold it in your mind, you can hold it in your hand.**

## The origin

My path into this work did not begin in a traditional laboratory.

I am originally from New Hampshire and studied Information Technology and Computer Information Systems at the University of Tampa and Southern New Hampshire University. My early professional background was in construction management, where I learned how to turn an idea into a plan, coordinate complex systems, solve real-world constraints, and carry a vision through to something physical.

The direction of my life changed after a profound experience in which I understood **One, or God, as speaking to me** and experienced a vision of reality as fundamentally connected. What followed was not a single idea, but years of writing, modeling, research, software development, mathematics, and system-building.

That experience became the origin of a larger body of work exploring **coherence, alignment, information, time, identity, life, intelligence, and the relationship between science and faith**.

The experience is the origin of the mission. It is not a substitute for evidence.

## The standard

A vision can begin the work. It cannot validate it.

Anything I place in the scientific or technical domain should be judged by what it can survive:

- Exact computation
- Independent reproduction
- Adversarial controls
- Explicit falsifiers
- Closed energy and information accounting
- Transparent limitations
- Published failures, not hidden ones

I would rather discover that an idea is wrong than protect it from a real test.

### Quantum-code discovery

[`qcode-discovery`](https://github.com/coherence-energy-labs/qcode-discovery) uses LLM-guided evolutionary search to discover and test bivariate bicycle and perturbed bivariate bicycle quantum LDPC codes.

The public work reports **465 distinct discovered codes**, exact MILP verification for key results, a documented rate-distance tradeoff, and cases where heuristic decoding substantially overestimated code distance.

[Explore the repository →](https://github.com/coherence-energy-labs/qcode-discovery)
